THE HAUNT

Dovercourt Crypt is a Halloween haunt in central Lynn Valley run by dedicated Halloween enthusiasts Mark Denny and Liz Naso - along with the indispensable help of their living dead-icated team of actors and volunteer crypt keepers! The Crypt has its modest origins as a simple graveyard display created by Mark nearly 15 years ago. Fuelled by a passion for crafting spine-tingling props, the display grew larger and more spectacular with each passing autumn. Finally, in 2014, the decision was made to transform the yard into an immersive walk-through experience - and thus, Dovercourt Crypt was born!
